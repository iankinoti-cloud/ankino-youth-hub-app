Why Ankino Hub?

Kenya has over 26 million people under 35 — the talent is here, the ambition is real. But too many young builders, creators, and founders are navigating the tech world alone: no mentors, no connections, no clear path.

That's what Ankino is solving. Founded by Ian Kinoti and powered by Safaricom's network, Ankino Youth Hub is the digital launchpad where Kenya's youth talent meets real infrastructure — from M-PESA micro-grants to Safaricom API access, from hackathon entry points to investor introductions.
